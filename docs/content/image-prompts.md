# Image Prompts - AutaMedica Content Calendar

Prompts para generar imagenes complementarias con Gemini/ChatGPT (DALL-E, Imagen).
Cada imagen acompana el thumbnail SVG del dia correspondiente.

---

## Dia 1 - "2 horas de espera"

**Prompt:**
A wide-angle photograph of a hospital waiting room at night, shot in a cinematic moody style. The room is dimly lit with harsh fluorescent lights casting cold blue-white tones. Rows of empty plastic chairs line the walls, with a single person sitting alone in the middle row, hunched over, staring at their phone. A large analog clock on the wall shows 2 hours have passed. The atmosphere is isolating and sterile. Color palette: desaturated blues, grays, and cold whites. No text. Shot as if from a documentary, 16:9 aspect converted to 9:16 vertical crop focusing on the lone patient and the clock. Photorealistic, high resolution.

## Dia 2 - "Before/After consulta"

**Prompt:**
A split-screen conceptual photograph in portrait orientation (9:16). Top half: A doctor's desk overwhelmed with chaos -- scattered paper files, sticky notes everywhere, an open laptop showing error screens, coffee stains on documents, and a stressed doctor's hands gripping their head (hands only visible, no face). The lighting is warm-orange and messy. Bottom half: The same desk, now immaculately organized -- a single clean tablet screen showing a structured patient summary with color-coded sections, a neat pen, and calm hands resting confidently on the table. The lighting is cool-blue and clean. A sharp horizontal line divides the two halves. Photorealistic, editorial photography style. No text or logos.

## Dia 3 - "Intenta romperlo"

**Prompt:**
A dramatic close-up photograph of a transparent glass shield standing upright on a dark matte surface, with red laser beams hitting it from multiple angles and scattering harmlessly. The shield has a subtle hexagonal pattern etched into it, glowing faintly blue at the impact points where the red beams are deflected. Behind the shield, a clean digital interface floats (holographic style) showing structured medical data. The background is pure black. The aesthetic is cybersecurity meets medical technology. Cinematic lighting with high contrast. Red and blue complementary color scheme. Photorealistic with slight sci-fi elements. Vertical 9:16 format. No text.

## Dia 4 - "El paciente ya habia dicho lo importante"

**Prompt:**
An intimate, warm-toned photograph of two hands across a table -- a patient's hands on one side, palms up, gesturing while speaking, and a doctor's hands on the other side, writing notes on a tablet. Between them, floating translucent speech bubbles (subtle digital overlay effect) transform from scattered, messy handwritten words on the patient's side into clean, organized bullet points on the doctor's side. The table surface is dark wood. Soft warm lighting from the side creates gentle shadows. The mood is empathetic and human. Shot in portrait orientation (9:16), tight crop on just the hands and the space between them. Photorealistic with minimal digital overlay. No faces visible.

---

## Dia 5 - "La agenda no estaba llena"

**Prompt:**
An overhead (bird's eye view) photograph of a large scheduling board or calendar grid on a dark desk surface. The calendar shows a week view with time slots. Most slots appear "full" with red and orange sticky notes crammed in, but when viewed carefully, there are obvious gaps and overlaps -- some slots have 3 stacked appointments while adjacent slots are completely empty. A hand with a blue pen is drawing arrows to reorganize the flow, with some slots now highlighted in clean cyan-blue showing an optimized distribution. The aesthetic is operational, systems-thinking. Dark desk surface, high contrast between the chaotic red notes and the optimized blue highlights. Vertical 9:16 format, shot from directly above. Photorealistic, editorial style. No text other than what naturally appears on sticky notes (can be blurred/illegible).
